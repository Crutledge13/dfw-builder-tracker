Overview


Experience something new on every level in this thoughtfully designed David Weekley home. Situated on a premium exterior homesite, this three-story home features three private patios, each offering breathtaking Trinity Tributary views.

 

The first floor welcomes you with a versatile retreat and covered patio, creating the perfect space for relaxing or hosting guests. On the second floor, the open-concept family room, dining area, streamlined kitchen, and sunlit study seamlessly connect to a private balcony. Escape to the third-floor Owner’s Retreat, complete with a contemporary en suite bath, walk-in closet, and an additional private patio to enjoy peaceful mornings or evening sunsets. 

 

Visit or contact David Weekley Homes at Trails at Waterside to experience elevated living, thoughtful design, and an exceptional lifestyle in one of Fort Worth's most desirable locations.
